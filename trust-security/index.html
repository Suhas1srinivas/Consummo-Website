Trust and Security

Trust and Security

Your planning data is business-critical. We treat it that way.

Demand, stock, pricing and supplier data are some of the most sensitive information a business holds. Conzmo is built to keep that data protected, private and under your control, at every step from your systems to your team.

Request a Demo

Our Security Commitments

The principles we follow to keep your data safe.

  • Data Protection

    Your data is protected with enterprise-grade encryption while it moves between systems and while it is stored.

  • Controlled Access

    Access is granted by role, so each person sees only the data they need, and your administrators stay in control.

  • Secure Data Handling

    Data is handled through secure processes designed for business-critical environments, from import to reporting.

  • Your Data Stays Yours

    Your data is used only to deliver your Conzmo service. We never sell it or share it with other customers.

  • Monitoring and Resilience

    The platform is monitored, and data is backed up, so your plans stay available when your teams need them.

  • Privacy by Design

    Privacy is considered from the start, and we collect only the data that is needed to plan your business.

How Your Data Flows

Your data is protected at every step, from your systems to your planners.

  1. Your systems

    Sales, stock, orders and master data from your ERP, POS and other systems.

    Encrypted
  2. Secure connection

    Data is transferred over encrypted connections into your Conzmo environment.

    Encrypted
  3. Conzmo platform

    Your data is stored securely and used only to plan your business.

    Encrypted
  4. Your team

    Planners see the data their role allows, in dashboards, reports and Nova.

    Role-based access

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about how Conzmo protects your data.

Who owns the data we share with Conzmo?

You do. Your data is used only to provide and improve your Conzmo service, and it is never sold to third parties.

How is our data protected?

Data is protected with enterprise-grade encryption, secure data handling and controlled access. Our team can share full technical details on request.

Who can see our data inside Conzmo?

Access is controlled by role, so people only see the data they need for their work. Your administrators decide who has access.

Can we get security documentation for our review?

Yes. Contact our team to request our security overview, data processing terms and help with your security questionnaire.

Where is our data stored?

Hosting and data location are agreed as part of your implementation. Our team can confirm the options available for your region.
